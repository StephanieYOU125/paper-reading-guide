English Paper Toolkit

英文論文定位句

Purpose

“This study aims to…”

“The objective of this paper is…”

“We investigate whether…”

Gap

“However, few studies have…”

“Previous studies have mainly focused on…”

“It remains unclear whether…”

Method

“We collected…”

“We employed / applied…”

“The model was trained using…”

Results

“The results indicate that…”

“We found a significant…”

“Compared with…, … showed…”

Limitations

“This study has several limitations.”

“The findings should be interpreted with caution…”

“Future research should…”

Critical reading

Is the conclusion supported by the data?

Would the result hold in another context?

What assumption does this method depend on?
